Accessed 2026-08-20. These timestamps are the comparison points used to define the
programmatic key poses. The videos are references for motion only; their frames are
not reproduced in the assets.

## Comparison result

The v5 movement axes and pose order were rebuilt from these points rather than from
the rejected v3/v4 images. Automated checks prove registration and mirroring only.
Visible anatomical side, head yaw/pitch/roll, pillow contact, and the Epley roll-to-rise
transition remain subject to clinician review and are not marked clinically approved.
