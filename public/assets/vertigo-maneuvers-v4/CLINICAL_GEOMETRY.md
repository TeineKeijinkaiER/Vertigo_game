# v4 Clinical Geometry Review

Status: review candidate only. Do not connect to the application until domain-owner approval.

## Priority

Clinical geometry outranks realism. A simplified pose is accepted only when the movement axis, side, head angle, support point, and body-to-table relationship are clear. No patient pose is rotated or skewed to fit a table.

## Dix-Hallpike, right

- Camera: centered elevated foot-end view; table long axis is screen vertical.
- Start: seated facing camera.
- Seated support: buttocks are on the table and both legs extend toward the camera along the table long axis; the patient is not standing or sitting on the floor in front of the table.
- Head turn: patient anatomical right is screen-left; nose points screen-left.
- Descent: straight backward along the screen-vertical table axis, not diagonally.
- Support: firm pillow is inboard from the head end under the shoulders/upper thorax. The occiput is not on the pillow.
- Final: target approximately 45 degrees of shoulder-supported head extension for this project, with the head lying headward of the pillow and right yaw preserved. Reference demonstrations using a smaller 20-30 degree extension are recorded as a different setup, not silently blended into this shoulder-pillow specification.
- Compared with Geeky Medics 00:40-00:55, Malcolm Hilton 00:20-00:45, and Fauquier ENT 00:10-00:20.

## Epley, right posterior canal

- Frames 1-3: seated neutral, right 45-degree yaw (nose screen-left), then backward descent with shoulder-supported head extension.
- Frames 1-2 keep identical on-table seated geometry; only the head turns.
- Frame 4: head-only turn across the midline; nose points screen-right while shoulders and pelvis remain supine.
- Frame 5: whole-body log roll to the left with the face/body directed screen-right and nose down. This is distinct from the preceding head-only turn.
- Frames 6-7: rise in the same screen-right-facing direction as frame 5; return the head to neutral without turning the torso back toward the camera.
- Compared with Michigan Medicine 00:10-01:05, Geeky Medics 01:15-01:35, ENT Information/Nimes 00:40-01:35, and Fauquier ENT 00:55-01:30.

## Gufoni, right geotropic

- Camera: straight frontal view; table long axis is screen horizontal.
- Start/end seating: buttocks rest on the table front edge, hips and knees are flexed, and lower legs hang vertically.
- Descent: toward the unaffected anatomical left, so the head moves from center to screen-right.
- Final side-lying pose: head turns nose down toward the table/floor.
- Compared with Fauquier ENT 00:52-01:25 and BPPV Made Easy 00:02-00:13, with Stanford/Michigan demonstrations as institutional support.

## Appiani / modified Gufoni, right apogeotropic

- Camera: same straight frontal view.
- Start/end seating: buttocks rest on the table front edge, hips and knees are flexed, and lower legs hang vertically.
- Descent: toward the affected anatomical right, so the head moves from center to screen-left.
- Final side-lying pose: head turns nose up away from the table/floor.
- Compared with Fauquier ENT 01:29-02:05 and BPPV Made Easy 00:02-00:17, with institutional demonstrations as support.
