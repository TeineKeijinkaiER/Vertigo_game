# Vertigo Maneuver Animation Research

Accessed 2026-08-21. Videos are used to compare movement sequence and anatomy; their frames, audio, branding, and participant likenesses are not reproduced.

## Dix-Hallpike test

- [Geeky Medics - Dix-Hallpike Test & Epley Manoeuvre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D6qEdlFVxig): 00:40 seated start, 00:45 head turn, 00:50 lowering, 00:55 observation position.
- [Malcolm Hilton - Dix-Hallpike test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEM9p4EX1jk): 00:10 seated start, 00:20 head turn, 00:35 lowering, 00:40-00:45 final head-hanging position.
- [Fauquier ENT - Dix-Hallpike Maneuver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgWOmuB1VFY): 00:10 seated start, 00:15 45-degree yaw, 00:20 approximately 30-degree neck extension.
- [Peter Johns / CMAJ - Dix-Hallpike and Epley](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kvVnEsGVLUY): 00:10-00:40 examination sequence.

Consensus used for the illustration: begin seated; rotate the head approximately 45 degrees toward the tested side; lower the trunk while preserving yaw; support the shoulders/head with approximately 20-30 degrees of neck extension; observe; return to sitting. For the right master viewed from in front, patient right is screen-left, so the nose must point screen-left after rotation.

## Gufoni and Appiani variants

- [Fauquier ENT - Gufoni Maneuver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N7LOSV-9DYE): geotropic example 00:52-01:25; ageotropic example 01:29-02:05.
- [BPPV Made Easy - Right Geotropic Gufoni](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVfyyw1o9gY): 00:03 seated, 00:04-00:05 unaffected-side descent, 00:06-00:07 nose down, 00:10-00:13 sitting.
- [BPPV Made Easy - Right Apogeotropic Gufoni/Appiani](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h-hvJLBQZHc): 00:02 seated, 00:04-00:06 affected-side descent, 00:07-00:09 nose up, 00:12-00:17 sitting.
- [Stanford Medicine - BPPV instructional videos](https://med.stanford.edu/ohns/OHNS-healthcare/earinstitute/our-services/dizziness-clinic/BPPV-Videos.html): institutional right and left Gufoni demonstrations.

Terminology varies between sources. This project keeps geotropic Gufoni and apogeotropic/Appiani as separate labeled sequences rather than blending them.
